# Code of Conduct

ApiGift projects are maintained as professional, respectful collaboration
spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on the work and technical outcomes.
- Assume good intent while communicating clearly.
- Do not share private information, credentials, or customer data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, and disclosure of private
information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when
behavior does not meet these standards.
